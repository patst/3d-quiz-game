# Playtests

Short, real sessions with children are the most important input for the game design.
Each playtest gets one file: `YYYY-MM-DD-<short-name>.md`, copied from `TEMPLATE.md`.
Agents read these files before planning the next milestone.

## Before the session (5 min)
- Phone or tablet charged, **sound on**, volume medium. Headphones are fine.
- Open https://patst.github.io/3d-quiz-game/ (or the PR preview link) once beforehand,
  so it is loaded.
- Create a fresh profile only if you want to watch the first-time flow.
- Have this checklist and the template open on a second device or on paper.

## During the session (10–15 min)
**Mostly watch, don't help.** Only step in if the child is stuck for ~30 s or gets
frustrated. Note *when* and *why* you helped — that is the most valuable data.

Things to watch:
1. **Start:** Does the child understand what to do after "Finde das Küken"?
   Picks an animal happily? Understands the profile screen next time?
2. **Moving:** Tap-to-walk or arrow keys — does it feel natural? Does the child tap far
   away and get confused when the avatar stops after a few steps?
3. **Camera / view:** Can the child see where to go? Is anything hidden by hedges or
   the buttons at the top?
4. **Chests:** Does the child walk to chests on its own? Is the floating star attractive?
5. **Challenges:**
   - Does the child listen to the question? Understand it without help?
   - Counting: counts correctly? Too many objects? Uses the hint numbers?
   - Addition: too easy / too hard? Uses the pictures?
   - Wrong answers: frustrated or motivated by "Fast! Versuch es nochmal"?
   - Does the child find/use "Überspringen"? Any accidental skips?
6. **Voice:** Understandable? Speed ok? Does the child like it? Annoying after a while?
7. **Stars:** Does the child notice the star counter? Care about it?
8. **Goal:** Happy when finding the chick? Wants another maze?
9. **Overall:** How long did the child play voluntarily? What did they say spontaneously?

## After the session (5 min)
Ask the child (keep it playful):
- "Was hat dir am besten gefallen?"
- "Was war doof oder schwer?"
- "Was soll es noch im Spiel geben?"

Fill in the template, commit it (or paste it into the chat and ask the agent to commit).
